# Niche marketplace candidates in Saudi Arabia with scattered supply/demand (as of Nov 2026)

**Read this first — evidence quality.** Research date 2026-11-14. Most figures below come from search-result summaries of the cited pages; only two pages were opened and read in full (Okaz 2199142 and Al-Eqtisadiah 16244). Haraj itself could not be browsed, so **no Haraj per-category listing counts were obtained**, and **no WhatsApp/Telegram/Snapchat group sizes were obtained** for any niche. Where a "dedicated platform exists" judgement rests only on App Store listings with few ratings, that is stated. Anything marked "prior knowledge, unverified" is my own recollection and not sourced in this session.

## Key Question 1: Which categories have very active informal trade without a strong dedicated platform?

### Takeaway
The best-evidenced "big but fragmented" categories are livestock (39M head; many tiny apps, none dominant), home businesses / الأسر المنتجة (133k+ certificates, SAR 22.5bn cumulative sales), used car parts / تشليح (several low-traction apps), used restaurant and café equipment (only general classifieds), falcons outside the official club auction, and winter camping/camp rental. Istiraha/chalet booking and private tutoring are already crowded with dedicated platforms and should not be ranked highly.

### Cited Findings

**Baseline — the general classifieds incumbents**
- Haraj's App Store listing claims 50M+ monthly visitors and includes used spare parts and tyres among its sections (company claim) — [App Store: Haraj](https://apps.apple.com/app/id662557215)
- Semrush estimated haraj.com.sa at 10.82M monthly visits in Oct 2025 and ranked it the most-visited automotive site in KSA (third-party estimate; conflicts with the 50M company claim, which may count app traffic) — [Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/website/top/saudi-arabia/vehicles/)
- A Great Place to Work profile claims 1.7M+ daily active users for Haraj (company-linked claim) — [GPTW](https://greatplacetowork.me/certified-organization/haraj/)
- Wamda (2016, dated) reported Haraj had ~30 staff, SAR 400M monthly sales through the platform in 2015, and that listings included sheep, cows and birds — [Wamda](https://www.wamda.com/en/2016/05/ksa-car-seller-haraj-turns-down-20m)
- Resale in KSA is described as mostly informal, via Haraj, Soum, Kayishha, Syarah, Dubizzle, OpenSooq, with trust and direct negotiation central; KSA re-commerce projected at USD 1.75bn in 2025 (+15.8%/yr) — [Sharikat Mubasher](https://www.sharikatmubasher.com/media-hub/experts-thoughts/18877)
- Mstaml (mstaml.com) is another active general classifieds site carrying listings for restaurant equipment, heavy equipment rental, tent/camp rental — [Mstaml example](https://www.mstaml.com/product/%D8%AA%D8%A3%D8%AC%D9%8A%D8%B1-%D8%AE%D9%8A%D8%A7%D9%85-%D9%88%D9%85%D8%B3%D8%AA%D9%84%D8%B2%D9%85%D8%A7%D8%AA%D9%87%D8%A7-%D9%81%D9%8A-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B1%D9%8A%D8%A7%D8%B6-%D8%A8%D8%B3%D8%B9%D8%B1-2500-%D8%B1%D9%8A%D8%A7%D9%84-%D8%B3%D8%B9%D9%88%D8%AF%D9%8A)

**Niche 1 — Livestock (sheep, goats, camels)**

**Niche 2 — Falcons and falconry gear**

- Hafar Al-Batin falcon market: 1,500+ birds in one site, 1,000+ falconers in the governorate alone — [Ajel](https://ajel.sa/local/sx7k4h)
- Buying season is tied to the approach of winter / hunting season — [Al Arabiya, Oct 2025](https://www.alarabiya.net/saudi-today/2025/10/04/%D8%B9%D9%88%D8%A7%D8%A6%D8%AF-%D9%85%D9%84%D9%8A%D9%88%D9%86%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D9%84%D8%AA%D8%B1%D8%A8%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B5%D9%82%D9%88%D8%B1-%D9%81%D9%8A-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B3%D8%B9%D9%88%D8%AF%D9%8A%D8%A9)

**Niche 3 — Productive families / home businesses (الأسر المنتجة)**
- 133,000+ productive-family certificates issued in 2025 (National Transformation Program annual report); cumulative sales of Social Development Bank-supported families SAR 22.5bn+ — [Al-Eqtisadiah, 9 Nov 2026](https://www.aleqt.com/الأخبار/المشاريع-المنزلية-السعودية-تقتحم-الاقتصاد-الرقمي-نحو-شرائح-أوسع-من-العملاء-16244)

### Inferences
- "Dedicated apps exist but none is strong" is the common pattern in livestock, تشليح, dresses and istiraha (Gathern aside): the App Store is full of near-zero-rating clones. That shows the idea is obvious and that building the app is not the hard part — seller acquisition and liquidity are. A new entrant needs a wedge other than "an app for X".
- The thinness of OpenSooq categories (96 restaurant-equipment listings, 13 tailoring listings) suggests this trade is not on classifieds at all and lives on Haraj, Instagram/Snapchat and WhatsApp — consistent with the brief's premise, but not directly measured here.
- Prior knowledge, unverified: Gathern is the dominant istiraha/chalet booking platform; Soum dominates used electronics; these were not confirmed with data in this session.

### Gaps
- Haraj listing counts per category (livestock, falcons, تشليح, equipment) — Haraj not browsable in this session; scraper products exist on Apify but were not run.

- Used kids' items, collectibles, bird/pet trade: no Saudi-specific evidence found either way.
- Content of the Jan 2025 livestock sale regulation (403 on source).
- Whether the small apps named above are still live in Nov 2026.

## Key Question 2: Evidence of size and pain points (fraud, price opacity, discoverability, no reviews)

### Takeaway
Advance-deposit ("عربون"/transfer-before-delivery) fraud on classifieds and social ads is the best-documented pain point, but no official Saudi statistic specific to Haraj or to any niche was found. Livestock has the clearest documented quality-opacity problem; home businesses have a documented marketing-capability gap.

### Cited Findings
- Saudi Public Prosecution: promoting fraudulent goods on social media carries up to 3 years' prison and/or SAR 1M fine (2021) — [Arab News](https://www.arabnews.pk/node/1879591)
- Saudi Ministry of Commerce has warned shoppers about online scams (2021) — [Arab News](https://www.arabnews.pk/node/1835416)
- Saudi cybersecurity federation: scammer activity rises 200%+ in shopping seasons (general, not classifieds-specific) — [Okaz](https://www.okaz.com.sa/economy/na/2254959)
- A commercial blog claims fraud reports on Haraj/OLX/Facebook Marketplace rose 35%+ in 2025 (no primary source; unreliable) — [lock.pub](https://lock.pub/ar/blog/kleinanzeigen-scam-prevention)
- Livestock: sellers in traditional markets hiding animal defects; online stores counter with video documentation and vet reports — [Okaz](https://www.okaz.com.sa/news/local/2199142)
- Livestock/pet ad fraud pattern (UAE police, not KSA): fake discounted adahi ads on social/messaging apps, fake certificates, deposit by bank transfer, untraceable numbers — [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/uae/people/eid-al-adha-2025-abu-dhabi-police-warn-of-fake-livestock-ads-charity-scams-1.500123291), [The National](https://www.thenationalnews.com/uae/government/2021/07/25/pets-for-sale-swindle-abu-dhabi-police-issue-warning-of-fake-web-ads)
- Falcons: the official auction markets itself as the "trusted and safe" channel and bans cash, with e-tagging and documentation — implying the off-auction market lacks those — [SFC](https://sfc.org.sa/sa/en/news/331)
- Home businesses: weak marketing/management skills and packaging/printing costs — [Al-Eqtisadiah](https://www.aleqt.com/الأخبار/المشاريع-المنزلية-السعودية-تقتحم-الاقتصاد-الرقمي-نحو-شرائح-أوسع-من-العملاء-16244)
- Regulatory context: the Saudi Business Center platform is the only approved channel for e-store verification; it requires a commercial registration or freelance document plus a commercial bank account. In a 2025 ministry assessment of 100 e-stores, store verification was the weakest criterion (75–79% compliance) — [Ministry of Commerce](https://mc.gov.sa/ar/mediacenter/News/Pages/09-09-25-01.aspx), [Okaz](https://www.okaz.com.sa/economy/saudi/2212620)

### Inferences
- Because the documented fraud mode is deposit-before-delivery, the cheapest trust feature a solo developer can offer is not escrow (payment licensing — avoid) but verified seller identity/history, reviews, and "pay on inspection" norms.
- Verification requirements (CR or freelance document) mean many informal sellers are unverified; a directory that shows who is verified could be valuable but also means a platform hosting unverified sellers carries some compliance exposure — needs legal check.

### Gaps
- No Saudi official fraud statistics by channel or category.
- No Saudi-specific reported cases of falcon/bird/pet sale fraud found (the cases found are from the UAE).
- Price-transparency complaints are inferred, not evidenced, for تشليح and restaurant equipment.

## Key Question 3: Which niches combine high frequency + meaningful ticket + reachable sellers?

### Takeaway
No niche scores well on all three with hard evidence; on the available facts, used restaurant/café equipment, تشليح parts requests, and camp/camping-gear rental look most workable for a solo developer, while livestock and home businesses are the largest but hardest to win. This ranking is my inference, not a sourced finding.

### Cited Findings
- Ticket-size evidence found: falcons ~SAR 98k average at auction, up to SAR 500k — [SFC](https://sfc.org.sa/sa/en/news/322); camp rental SAR 1,200–3,000/day and tent sets SAR 2,500 — [Al-Watan](https://www.alwatan.com.sa/article/1034803), [Mstaml](https://www.mstaml.com/product/%D8%AA%D8%A3%D8%AC%D9%8A%D8%B1-%D8%AE%D9%8A%D8%A7%D9%85-%D9%88%D9%85%D8%B3%D8%AA%D9%84%D8%B2%D9%85%D8%A7%D8%AA%D9%87%D8%A7-%D9%81%D9%8A-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B1%D9%8A%D8%A7%D8%B6-%D8%A8%D8%B3%D8%B9%D8%B1-2500-%D8%B1%D9%8A%D8%A7%D9%84-%D8%B3%D8%B9%D9%88%D8%AF%D9%8A); tutoring platform take rates 15–30% — [Al-Watan](https://www.alwatan.com.sa/article/1170028)
- Seller-base evidence found: 133k+ certified productive families — [Al-Eqtisadiah](https://www.aleqt.com/الأخبار/المشاريع-المنزلية-السعودية-تقتحم-الاقتصاد-الرقمي-نحو-شرائح-أوسع-من-العملاء-16244); 1,000+ falconers in Hafar Al-Batin alone — [Ajel](https://ajel.sa/local/sx7k4h)

### Inferences (all rows below are my assessment; ticket/frequency without a citation above are unsourced estimates and are left qualitative)

| Niche | Sellers → Buyers | Ticket | Frequency | Seller reachability | Existing dedicated platform | Solo-dev fit |

## Key Question 4: Which niches are seasonal?

### Takeaway
Falcons, camping/camp rental, dates and adahi livestock are strongly seasonal on cited evidence; textbooks, tutoring and weddings are seasonal by inference only.

### Cited Findings
- Falcons: local auction runs 1 Oct–30 Nov; buying peaks before winter hunting season — [SFC](https://sfc.org.sa/sa/en/news/331), [Al Arabiya](https://www.alarabiya.net/saudi-today/2025/10/04/%D8%B9%D9%88%D8%A7%D8%A6%D8%AF-%D9%85%D9%84%D9%8A%D9%88%D9%86%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D9%84%D8%AA%D8%B1%D8%A8%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B5%D9%82%D9%88%D8%B1-%D9%81%D9%8A-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B3%D8%B9%D9%88%D8%AF%D9%8A%D8%A9)
- Camping: winter is the selling/renting season for camps — [Ajel](https://ajel.sa/local/tfz9ky)
- Dates: Buraidah carnival trades in August (SAR 98M in the first 15 days of Aug 2026) — [Ajel](https://ajel.sa/economy/l9m59pb2su)
- Livestock: online sales spike around Eid al-Adha — [Okaz](https://www.okaz.com.sa/news/local/2199142)
- General: fraud activity rises 200%+ in shopping seasons — [Okaz](https://www.okaz.com.sa/economy/na/2254959)

### Inferences
- Year-round (inferred): تشليح parts, restaurant equipment, home businesses (with Ramadan/Eid peaks), event rental (with summer/holiday wedding peaks).
- Semester-bound (inferred): textbooks (start of each term), tutoring (exam periods).
- Research date is mid-November: camping and falcon seasons are underway now, so a camping-rental test could be run immediately, whereas a dates or adahi product would wait until mid-2027.

- No month-by-month demand data (search trends, listing volumes) retrieved for any niche.

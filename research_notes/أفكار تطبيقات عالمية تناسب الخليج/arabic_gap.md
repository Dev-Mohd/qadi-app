# Arabic / Saudi-Gulf localization gap map for proven global app categories (as of Nov 2026)

Reading notes for the report writer:
- Research date: 14 Nov 2026. Older figures are flagged with their year.
- "Nothing found" below means my searches (English + Arabic, roughly 30 queries) surfaced nothing. It is NOT proof that nothing exists. I did not systematically browse the Saudi App Store / Google Play charts, and I could not obtain ratings counts for most apps.
- Many figures were read from search-result summaries, not from the full page. Those are marked "(snippet)". Only the Sensor Tower/Bidease report was read in full text.
- Excluded by brief: perfumes, tourism-experience marketplaces, fintech/payments, food/grocery delivery. Fintech players are named only where they occupy an adjacent category (kids' allowance).

## 1. For each category, what are the leading Arabic/Gulf apps, how big are they, and are they well-rated or neglected?

### Takeaway
Funded Arabic/Gulf incumbents exist in kids' Arabic education, Quran/prayer, therapy and meditation, exam prep, podcasts, gifting commerce, chalet/estiraha booking, voice-chat social and Arabic AI chat. I found no strong Arabic-native product in habit/accountability with friends, family organizer/shared calendar, kids' routines (non-fintech), friend-making for Saudis, women-only communities, Saudi neighbourhood apps, family elder-care coordination, hobby communities, or coordination of gatherings among friends (as opposed to venue booking).

### Inferences
- The funded Arabic incumbents cluster where content or supply is the moat (kids' content, therapists, tutors, florists, chalets, podcasts). The empty categories are small-group coordination tools (family, friends, shilla/majlis, habit groups), which in the Gulf are probably absorbed by WhatsApp groups rather than by any app. That makes them open but also hard to monetise.
- Categories a solo unfunded developer should treat as closed on competition grounds: Quran/prayer, gifting commerce, exam-prep content, therapy marketplaces, general Arabic AI chat, podcasts.
- Most plausible solo-developer openings on this evidence: Arabic-first habit/accountability for small friend or family groups; family organizer with Hijri calendar and occasions; non-fintech kids' routines; gathering coordination (who brings what, who is coming, cost tally without payments); Gulf-dialect learning content for expats. All of these are inferences from absence of evidence, not demonstrated demand.

### Gaps
- No ratings counts or current download figures were obtained for most apps; store pages were not fetched individually. "Well-rated vs neglected" is therefore largely unanswered.
- Gathern's current scale and later funding, Tuhoon's user numbers, Lamsa's current status (most Lamsa data is 2016-2020), and HUMAIN Chat usage were not found.
- Not searched in depth: coffee, car and gaming communities; women-only apps in Arabic; dedicated Qudurat question-bank apps; Arabic journaling apps; Ramadan-specific habit apps. Absence here is weak evidence.
- Kaleela, AlifBee, Abjadiyat, Kutubee, Asafeer were queried but returned nothing; their status is unknown, not non-existent.

## 2. What documented examples exist of a global app idea successfully localized/cloned for MENA (and failures)? What made the difference?

### Takeaway
The clearest documented successes replaced a text/visual global format with a culturally native one (Yalla's voice "majlis" rooms, Labayh/Tuhoon's dialect content, Hawaya's family-aware matchmaking), while the clearest failure signal is that even a Match Group-backed localized app (Hawaya) was shut down. Hard causal evidence on what "made the difference" is thin; most available commentary is vendor marketing.

### Cited Findings
- Yalla Group built a voice-chat and casual-games business for MENA with US$341.9M revenue in 2025 — [WAM](https://www.wam.ae/en/article/bz4tll6-yalla-group-reports-revenues-aed1256-billion-2025); MAU spikes around Ramadan and the company then shifted acquisition to higher-engagement users (snippet) — [Insider Monkey Q2 2025 transcript](https://www.insidermonkey.com/blog/yalla-group-limited-nyseyala-q2-2025-earnings-call-transcript-1589787/)
- Hawaya (Egyptian "culturally acceptable" Muslim matchmaking app, acquired by Match Group in 2019, expanded to UAE, Kuwait, Saudi Arabia) was discontinued on 15 Feb 2023 — [Global Dating Insights](https://www.globaldatinginsights.com/news/hawaya-to-shut-down-in-february-2023/); it had reported four million users — [Global Dating Insights](https://www.globaldatinginsights.com/news/hawaya-reaches-four-million-users-following-app-update)
- Tuhoon differentiates on Gulf Arabic dialect and locally developed clinical content — [Fast Company ME](https://fastcompanyme.com/recommenders/five-apps-making-mental-health-more-accessible-in-the-middle-east/)
- Labayh grew to 1.2M users with Ministry of Health accreditation and stigma-reduction campaigns — [Eye of Riyadh](https://www.eyeofriyadh.com/news/details/labayh-saudi-platform-exceeded-1-million-users-of-your-mental-health-matters-more-initiative-to-raise-awareness-and-address-mental-illness-stigma)
- Local shopping apps held their download share in Saudi Arabia despite Temu, SHEIN and AliExpress entering — [Sensor Tower/Bidease](https://investgame.net/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/the_2025_middle_east_app_growth_report.pdf)
- Hayi explicitly cloned the Nextdoor model for the UAE with resident verification and reached about 30,000 users in roughly four years — [Wamda](https://www.wamda.com/2024/02/social-app-hayi-raises-undisclosed-seed-round)
- Localization lifts installs: Pocket Gems tripled installs from Arabic speakers after localizing War Dragons (2017) — [Android Developers Blog](https://android-developers.googleblog.com/2017/05/why-you-should-localize-your-app-or.html?hl=id)
- Vendor guidance (low evidential weight): RTL requires mirroring navigation, icons and animations, not only text; many international apps still deliver poor Arabic UX; mada is the dominant Saudi payment method, with STC Pay and Apple Pay widely used — [MarkupDesigns](https://www.markupdesigns.com/blog/english-arabic-app-development-middle-east/); [TrustRadio Gulf localization guide](https://trustradio.com.ng/en/the-no-bs-guide-to-app-localization-in-the-gulf-2026-edition)
- Saudi weddings are gender-segregated, which changes event-product requirements — [Wamda, 2015](https://www.wamda.com/en/2015/06/has-saying-i-do-in-ksa-just-become-a-whole-lot-easier-)
- Riyadh newcomers rely on WhatsApp groups and offline classes to build social circles — [Cat in KSA (Substack)](https://catinksa.substack.com/p/five-ways-i-built-community-abroad)

### Inferences
- Voice and audio fit the region better than text-heavy formats (Yalla, Thmanyah, HUMAIN voice input); a new product for Gulf users should probably be voice-note friendly.
- A neighbourhood clone reaching only ~30,000 users in four years with VC money suggests that location-based "community" apps are slow to grow in the Gulf; a solo developer should not expect network effects from geography alone.
- For a solo developer, in-app purchase via Apple/Google already covers Apple Pay and local cards; direct mada/STC Pay integration matters mainly for web or physical-goods flows. This is my reasoning, not a sourced finding.
- Family-centred, gender-aware and religious-calendar design are repeatedly named as differentiators in the sources, but I found no controlled evidence quantifying their effect.

### Gaps
- No reliable source was found on Clubhouse's rise and fall among Saudi users, on Careem/Anghami/Jawaker as cloning case studies, or on documented failures of Western family/habit apps in Arabic. These were not searched in depth.
- No quantified evidence on the conversion impact of dialect vs Modern Standard Arabic, Hijri calendar support, or women-only modes.
- Why Hawaya was closed beyond the company's stated "focus on other services" is not documented.

## 3. What do data sources say about Saudi/Gulf app usage and spending by category?

### Takeaway
The GCC app market is growing faster than the global average (IAP above $700M per quarter, +20% YoY), with spending concentrated in entertainment, AI software and finance. Health & Wellness, Education and Religion are all top-ten genres with double-digit IAP growth, but marketers describe KSA and UAE as saturated. Saudi-only category totals and Saudi subscription conversion rates were not found.

### Cited Findings
All from the Sensor Tower/Bidease "2025 Middle East App Growth Report" (Fall 2025; store data Jan 2023 to Jun 2025; survey of 425 marketers, Aug 2025) unless stated — [report PDF](https://investgame.net/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/the_2025_middle_east_app_growth_report.pdf)
- GCC downloads rose 2.6% YoY in Q2 2025 vs 0.5% globally.
- GCC IAP revenue passed $700M in Q2 2025, +20% YoY vs 13% globally; UAE +26%.
- Time spent in apps passed 20 billion hours in Q2 2025, +4% YoY.
- Download growth by genre, H1 2025 vs H1 2024: Financial Services +34%, Food & Dining +31%, Law & Government +17%, Health & Wellness +14%, Jobs & Education +7%, Media & Entertainment +7%, Travel +7%, Religion & Spirituality +6%, Software +5%, Shopping -7%.
- IAP revenue growth by genre, H1 2025: Software +77%, Financial Services +57%, Shopping +35%, Auto +25%, Lifestyle & Services +22%, Media & Entertainment +18%, Travel +18%, Health & Wellness +17%, Jobs & Education +16%, Religion & Spirituality +14%.
- ChatGPT was #3 by revenue in the region in H1 2025 behind TikTok and Shahid.
- Shopping downloads spike more than 30% around Ramadan and Black Friday; finance activity in KSA peaks at month-end with the salary cycle.
- 52% of surveyed marketers name saturation in KSA and UAE as the top challenge; 61% see new seasonal windows emerging.
- Other sources: WhatsApp is the most-used app in Saudi Arabia per the CST report (snippet) — [Minute Mirror](https://minutemirror.com.pk/whatsapp-is-most-used-app-in-saudi-arabia-report-finds-620330/); CST data reported as WhatsApp 93% usage and 99.6% internet penetration (snippet) — [Al Watan](https://www.alwatan.com.sa/ampArticle/1183380)
- Snapchat reaches 26 million people in Saudi Arabia, per Snap's CEO at FII9 — [Saudi Gazette](https://saudigazette.com.sa/article/656090)
- In Middle East and Africa, monthly subscription plans are 55% of the mix; iOS concentrates higher willingness to pay; games trials convert at 19% (AppsFlyer data as reported, snippet) — [Martechvibe](https://martechvibe.com/article/middle-east-subscription-app-growth-shifts-across-categories-and-platforms)
- A $9.99/month subscription comes to roughly SAR 37.99 including Saudi VAT (practitioner guide, not measured data) — [AppsOps](https://appsops.store/blog/app-store-pricing-mena-saudi-uae-egypt-2026)
- Bumble's Saudi listing peaked at about $40K revenue in early December 2025 (snippet; period unit unclear) — [Sensor Tower](https://sensortower.com/blog/2025-q4-unified-top-5-lifestyle-revenue-sa-600b3087241bc16eb8090944)

### Inferences
- With WhatsApp near-universal and Snapchat at roughly three quarters of the population, any community product competes with, or must hand off to, those two. Sharing into WhatsApp/Snapchat is likely the only free acquisition channel for a developer with no audience.
- A monthly-plan preference plus prices near SAR 20-40 suggests a low-priced monthly tier is safer than annual-only paywalls.
- "Saturation" in the survey refers to paid user acquisition among funded apps; it reinforces that a solo developer cannot buy growth and needs a built-in sharing loop.

### Gaps
- No Saudi-only genre totals (Sensor Tower publishes GCC aggregates; country dashboards are paywalled).
- No Saudi-specific trial-to-paid or subscription retention benchmarks (RevenueCat/Adapty country breakdowns not found).
- GASTAT ICT household survey and the CST "Saudi Internet" report were not read directly; the WhatsApp and internet figures above are second-hand.
- data.ai and Statista category spending figures were not accessible.

## 4. Which social problems in Saudi/Gulf society are documented with data and could be addressed by an app/community?

### Takeaway
Officially quantified problems include physical inactivity (especially women), smoking, youth and female unemployment, mental health prevalence, and a volunteering system that has already exceeded its Vision 2030 target. Children's screen time and loneliness are documented only by small academic studies, and divorce statistics are inconsistent across sources.

### Cited Findings
- Physical activity: 59.1% of adults 18+ do at least 150 minutes per week; men 66.5%, women 43.1% (GASTAT 2025, self-reported) — [Saudi Gazette](https://cdn.saudigazette.com.sa/article/655959); a youth sedentary trend is noted alongside a 58.5% adult figure — [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/world/gulf/saudi/saudi-arabia-585-of-adults-active-but-youth-sedentary-habits-on-the-rise-1.1733140777697)
- Obesity: 23.1% of those aged 15+ per GASTAT's self-reported 2024 survey, cited by a secondary site that notes independent estimates are much higher (snippet, unverified against GASTAT) — [vision2030.ai](https://vision2030.ai/tags/demographics/)
- Smoking: adult current smoking 16.6% in 2024, men 24.3% (snippet) — [GSTHR](https://gsthr.org/countries/profile/SAU/1/); primary source — [GASTAT Health Determinants 2024](https://www.stats.gov.sa/documents/20117/2435259/Health+Determinants+Statistics+Publication+2024+EN.pdf); contradicted by 14.7% on [Macrotrends](https://www.macrotrends.net/countries/SAU/saudi-arabia/smoking-rate-statistics)
- Unemployment: Saudi nationals 7.2% in Q4 2025; Saudi women 10.3% — [Zawya](https://www.zawya.com/en/economy/gcc/unemployment-rate-among-saudis-drops-to-72-in-q4-2025-yipy0ver); overall 3.5% — [Arab News](https://www.arabnews.com/node/2638289); youth rate 12.3% in Q4 2025 (snippet) — [Trading Economics](https://tradingeconomics.com/saudi-arabia/youth-unemployment-rate)
- Mental health: 34% lifetime prevalence per the Saudi National Mental Health Survey as reported — [Eye of Riyadh](https://www.eyeofriyadh.com/news/details/34-of-saudis-are-diagnosed-with-mental-health-disorders-as-per-saudi-national-mental-health-survey)
- Social media and loneliness: among 978 young adults at three public universities (Jan-Jun 2024), 52.8% met criteria for social media addiction, and loneliness and fear of missing out were significant predictors (snippet) — [PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12710093/)
- Elderly loneliness: 80% of sampled older adults in Jazan reported moderate loneliness (2022 data collected under COVID restrictions) — [PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11675458)
- Children's screen time: 68.2% of 6-12-year-olds in an Eastern Province sample had 6+ hours per day (2022, small sample) — [PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9675307); a Saudi/Bahrain study of 6-14-year-olds found 7.25 hours on weekdays and 8.4 on weekends (snippet) — [PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11549943)
- Divorce: more than 350,000 divorced women recorded in GASTAT's 2022 Saudi Women's Report (a stock of divorced women, not annual cases, as I read it) — [The National](https://www.thenationalnews.com/gulf-news/2023/08/14/saudi-arabia-reports-more-than-350000-couples-got-divorced-in-2022); about 57,595 divorce cases reported for 2025, 157 per day (snippet; the same number appears elsewhere attributed to 2020, so treat with caution) — [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/world/gulf/saudi/short-lived-marriages-on-the-rise-in-saudi-arabia-as-65-of-divorces-occur-within-first-year-1.500195831); "7 divorces every hour, 3 per 10 marriages" (older) — [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/world/gulf/saudi/saudi-arabia-sees-7-divorce-cases-every-hour-1.85135151)
- Volunteering: Vision 2030 target of 1 million volunteers a year passed ahead of schedule — [Arab News](https://arabnews.com/node/2581931/business-economy); 1.5 million registered in 2025 (snippet) — [SceneNow](https://scenenow.com/News/Saudi-Arabia-Reaches-1-5-Million-Registered-Volunteers-in-2025); volunteer hours rose from 53M (2023) to 80M (2024) (snippet) — [Goodera](https://www.goodera.com/ebook/corporate-volunteering-in-saudi-arabia-2025)
- Ageing: GASTAT-based figure of roughly 1.3 million seniors today (snippet) — [Frontiers in Public Health](https://www.frontiersin.org/articles/10.3389/fpubh.2024.1446360/full); Ministry of Health aims to raise annual home-care coverage from 35,000 (2019) to 145,000 by 2030 (snippet) — [World Health Expo](https://www.worldhealthexpo.com/insights/healthcare-management/growth-opportunities-in-specialised-sectors-of-saudi-arabia); context — [PwC GCC ageing report, 2024](https://www.pwc.com/m1/en/publications/documents/2024/longevity-and-ageing-populations-in-gcc-countries-.pdf)
- Podcast audience skewing female (2020) — [MEPRA / markettiers](https://www.mepra.org/knowledge/news/first-of-its-kind-study-reveals-there-are-5-1-million-regular-podcast-listeners-in-saudi-arabia-and-women-are-leading-the-charge/)

### Inferences
- The 23-point gender gap in physical activity is the best-quantified problem that a small, women-friendly accountability group product could address without clinical claims.
- Volunteering is a poor target: the target is already exceeded and the government platform owns registration and hour certification.
- Children's screen time and family routines are a real concern in the literature, but the supporting data are small regional samples; present them as indicative only.
- Loneliness after relocating to Riyadh is supported only by journalism and expat forums in what I found, not by prevalence data.

### Gaps
- No national loneliness prevalence figure for Saudi adults, and nothing specific to internal migrants to Riyadh.
- No verified 2024/2025 GASTAT divorce count; published "divorce rate" figures conflict because they measure different things.
- GASTAT obesity and smoking figures were not read from the primary publication.
- A GASTAT news listing dated 22 Oct 2026 appears to give 59.9% for adult physical activity; I could not confirm it on the official page.
- No official national children's screen-time statistic was found.

## 5. Which categories are culturally or legally sensitive and should be avoided or handled carefully by a solo developer?

### Takeaway
Dating/matchmaking, open mixed-gender stranger matching, clinical mental-health claims and products that collect children's personal data carry the highest cultural and legal risk; the PDPL requires verified guardian consent for minors and documented consent generally. Evidence on enforcement against small apps is absent.

### Cited Findings
- PDPL Executive Regulations: when relying on a legal guardian's consent, the controller must take appropriate measures to verify the guardianship, and consent must be documented so it can be verified later — [Bird & Bird, Children in the Digital World: Saudi Arabia](https://childreninthedigitalworld.twobirds.com/home/saudi-arabia); [Baker McKenzie](https://resourcehub.bakermckenzie.com/en/resources/global-data-and-cyber-handbook/emea/saudi-arabia/topics/legal-bases-for-processing-of-personal-data)
- Age threshold is inconsistent across commentators: under 18 per Bird & Bird (via other Saudi law) vs under 13 per vendor guidance — [Bird & Bird](https://childreninthedigitalworld.twobirds.com/home/saudi-arabia); [Cookiebot](https://www.cookiebot.com/en/saudi-arabia-personal-data-protection-law-pdpl/)
- Practice guides call for verifiable parental consent and extra care against profiling or marketing to minors — [Chambers Data Protection & Privacy 2026: Saudi Arabia](https://practiceguides.chambers.com/practice-guides/data-protection-privacy-2026/saudi-arabia)
- Local hosting is presented as a PDPL compliance feature by HUMAIN Chat — [W.Media](https://w.media/humain-chat-saudi-arabia-launches-its-own-arabic-first-ai-app/)
- Matchmaking is commercially fragile even when localized: Hawaya closed in 2023 — [Global Dating Insights](https://www.globaldatinginsights.com/news/hawaya-to-shut-down-in-february-2023/). Global dating apps nonetheless earn revenue in Saudi Arabia (Bumble, snippet) — [Sensor Tower](https://sensortower.com/blog/2025-q4-unified-top-5-lifestyle-revenue-sa-600b3087241bc16eb8090944)
- Stranger-meeting apps position themselves explicitly as "not dating" in Riyadh coverage — [Arab News](https://arabnews.com/node/2587429/saudi-arabia)
- Mental-health platforms that succeed locally carry Ministry of Health accreditation and licensed practitioners (Labayh) — [Yespress](https://yespress.io/labayh)
- Marketers cite limited first-party data (44%) and platform privacy changes as headwinds in the region — [Sensor Tower/Bidease](https://investgame.net/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/the_2025_middle_east_app_growth_report.pdf)

### Inferences
- Lowest-risk design for a solo developer: closed groups of people who already know each other (family, friends, classmates), no stranger discovery, no public profiles or photos required, adults as account holders with children represented only as parent-managed profiles, and "wellbeing/habit" framing rather than diagnosis or treatment claims.
- Avoid: dating or marriage matching, open mixed-gender discovery, therapy or diagnosis, direct child accounts, and anything requiring a licence (health, finance, charity fundraising).
- Women-only communities look open commercially but need identity/gender verification and moderation, which is heavy for one person.

### Gaps
- I found no source on the specific legal status of dating apps, on content-licensing requirements for social/community apps (Ministry of Media / GAMR, CST), or on any need for a Saudi commercial registration to publish; these need direct legal checking.
- No evidence on PDPL enforcement actions against small or foreign app developers.
- Rules on charitable fundraising and volunteer coordination outside official platforms were not researched.
